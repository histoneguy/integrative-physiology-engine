Integrative Physiology Engine

A whole-body human model assembled from primary literature. Arterial pressure is an output of the closed loop, never a setpoint. Every figure on this page came out of a model run; every parameter behind it carries its citation.

Resting steady state — 70 kg adult, 205 mmol/day sodium

Integrated to day 400 and stationary there: relative drift between day 200 and day 400 is under 1 part in 109. The right-hand figure is the same model with the sexed parameters resolved to their female values (ADR 0014). Quantities with no sex dependence are identical by construction — shown anyway, because that is where a leak between the two would become visible.

Parameter ledger

reported — read from a measurement in a source. derived — arithmetic on other rows. assumed — no admissible source could be opened, and the search that failed is recorded on the row. calibrated — fitted, and the ledger is organised to drive this count to zero. Open any row for its full citation.

Structural relations

conservation and definitional relations need no citation — they need to be declared as such, which is a claim a reviewer can check. An empirical relation must carry a citation for its functional form, and a gate fails the build if one does not.
